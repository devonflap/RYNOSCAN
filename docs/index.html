Bid-ready charts

Coins where all 7 gates pass, whether the setup is a bottom reversal or a chart pattern. Each chart shows the entry zone (shaded), stop (red) and take-profits (green) over 4h candles with volume underneath; the purple dashed line is the pattern's neckline, rim or trend lines.

High risk, high reward runners

Coins with unusual volume, buyers leading, more wallets buying than selling and holders growing. Each card shows four checks. Coins that trip a risk rule (wallets selling, a few wallets driving the volume, a flagged token, thin liquidity) are dropped from the list but still tracked, so the data can show whether the rules help. These are not bid-ready charts: they have no tested entry rules and can fall 50% or more, or be rugged. Updated on each full scan.

Spot & chill meme setups

The Alts desk rules run over the meme universe on daily candles: a coin that has already run 100%+, is now correcting in a falling wedge, flag or flat base, and is close to resolving on a daily close. This is the chill method: the correction has to reach the 0.618 retracement of the last leg (purple lines mark 0.5 / 0.618 / 0.786 / 0.886), and a wedge whose low lands on a fib scores best. When volume is still rising on the way down, the range isn't tightening, or price is sitting on the low without a bounce, the card carries a warning that it may run deeper to 0.786 / 0.886 first: a judgement call for you, not a gate. Entry is the daily close through the red line; invalidation is the next fib down. Meme rules for the exit: small size, clip 20% into strength, moonbag the rest. Updated once a day after the UTC close.

Worth watching

Valid plan, $250k+ volume and close to bid-ready, ranked by opportunity score. Risk is shown, not capped: the stop sits wherever the setup is proved wrong, so check the stop distance before sizing.

Chart patterns and bottomed-out charts

Bottomed-out charts are coins that sold off to oversold, stopped making lower lows and are now turning up (an RSI flip, a divergence or a higher low). They are early: levels are indicative until all 7 gates pass. Chart patterns (bull flag, inverse head and shoulders, cup and handle, falling wedge, double bottom) are found on the 4h chart by fixed rules; the purple lines on each chart are the pattern itself.

Alts desk timing beats time · spot & chill

Market Watcher-style HTF spot setups on the top 80 alts by volume, daily candles, scanned once a day after the UTC close. Regime first (BTC and the alt basket above their weekly support), then coins that have already run 50%+ and are correcting in a falling wedge, bull flag or flat base. WATCH is a pattern forming; DECISION is price pressing the upper line; BID is a daily close through it, on volume. Entry is that close, invalidation sits under the pattern low, T1 is the impulse high and T2/T3 the measured move. Phone alerts fire only when a state changes. Relative strength, the spot book and the playbook are on the chips below.

Paper desk

Fifteen paper books, each with its own $10,000: conservative (bid-ready setups, and risky coins under three exit plans), the degen desk plus three other exit plans on the same coins (ladder, moonbag, and cut-on-sellers), three playbook setups (Fib pullback, inside the pattern, proven wallets), an early-move book fed by a 15-minute checker plus a "buy the flush" version of it, a breakout scalper and a random-entry baseline every book must beat. The Council tab is six rule-based reviewers that argue over the results and a chair that gives one final answer. Bot sim trades them through one risk-sized account. No real money is involved.
