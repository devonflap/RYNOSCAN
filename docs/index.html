Position sizing

Optional. Enter your account size and each card shows a suggested position: the smaller of what the stop distance allows at your risk per trade, and a share of the pool's liquidity (so your own buying and selling doesn't move the price). Saved in this browser only.

Bid-ready charts

Coins where all 7 gates pass, whether the setup is a bottom reversal or a chart pattern. Each chart shows the entry zone (shaded), stop (red) and take-profits (green) over 4h candles with volume underneath; the purple dashed line is the pattern's neckline, rim or trend lines.

Worth watching

Valid plan, $250k+ volume and close to bid-ready, ranked by opportunity score. Risk is shown, not capped: the stop sits wherever the setup is proved wrong, so check the stop distance before sizing.

Chart patterns and bottomed-out charts

Bottomed-out charts are coins that sold off to oversold, stopped making lower lows and are now turning up (an RSI flip, a divergence or a higher low). They are early: levels are indicative until all 7 gates pass. Chart patterns (bull flag, inverse head and shoulders, cup and handle, falling wedge, double bottom) are found on the 4h chart by fixed rules; the purple lines on each chart are the pattern itself.

    Exit rules

    Every plan on this page uses the same exits, so the decision to sell is made before you buy. Follow them in order.

    Stop lossExit everything

    Place it half an ATR (average 4h candle range) below the lowest low of the setup. If price closes through it, sell the whole position. The distance is on every card as "stop %" so you can size the position before you enter.

    Take profit 1Sell one third

    First target, never closer than 1R above entry. Then move the stop up to your entry price. The trade can no longer lose money.

    Take profit 2Sell one third

    Second target, always beyond TP1. A plan only counts if TP2 pays at least 2R, so the reward is at least twice the risk.

    Take profit 3Sell the rest

    The full target, always beyond TP2. This is the "upside to TP3" figure on each card.

    Entry window

    Buy only inside the shaded entry zone. If the zone isn't filled within 48 hours, the signal expires and you skip it.

    Chart-pattern trades

    Same ladder, measured from the pattern. The stop sits half an ATR under the pattern's low. TP1 is half the pattern's height above its trigger, TP2 the full height, TP3 one and a half times.

    Candle close

    Use the stop on a 4h close below the stop price. Do not widen a stop after you enter, and do not move it down.

    Time exit

    If the trade is still open 7 days after the fill with no target or stop hit, sell at market.

    Weak volume day

    When the volume gauge shows Poor or Below normal, stops slip and targets stall. Take no new trades, or one small one.

    Portfolio risk

    No more than 3 positions open at once, and risk about 1% of your account per trade (2% at the very most). Several meme coins fall together, so three positions is closer to one big bet than three small ones. Use the sizing panel above.

    Market backdrop

    When the banner says SOL or ETH is risk-off, bottom reversals fail more often. Size down or wait; do not average down.

    R means risk

    1R is the distance from entry to stop. A move to TP2 that is 2R pays twice what the stop would lose.

    Rule-based screen, not financial advice. The exit rules are untested until the signal log has about 30 closed signals. Meme coins can go to zero and stops can gap on thin liquidity.
